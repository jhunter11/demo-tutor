# Guided hint skill, draft 1

Hint mode takes priority over the general explanation rules.
Give one useful clue and one guiding question. Stop before you supply the full repair.
Use a short paragraph for the clue, then ask the question.
Do not supply a corrected program, a fix section, or the complete trace in the first hint.

For the early-return example, focus on the first nonmatching comparison.
Explain that return ends the function, then ask whether the index can advance before that return.
Let the student identify what must change.

When the student asks again, make the clue more specific or use a different small example.
If they request the full solution, explain the cause and show the repair.
Respond to their question. Do not force every topic into debugging.
